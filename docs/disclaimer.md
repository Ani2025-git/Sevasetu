# Independent Demonstration Project Disclaimer

**Platform Name:** SevaQueue  
**Tagline:** Know Before You Visit  
**Jurisdiction:** West Bengal, India  
**Pilot Target:** Demonstration Model for Bangla Sahayata Kendras (BSK)  

---

## 1. Strictly Independent Technological Prototype
SevaQueue is an **independent demonstration and civic research project**. 

> **NOTICE:**
> SevaQueue is NOT officially affiliated with, endorsed by, certified by, operated by, or connected to the **Government of West Bengal**, the **Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms (P&AR)**, or any official **Bangla Sahayata Kendra (BSK)**.

All logos, product representations, and references to Bangla Sahayata Kendra are used solely for simulated demonstration and academic workflow presentation purposes.

---

## 2. Queue Model: "Information Only"
SevaQueue operates strictly on an **"Evidence-Based Queue Information"** model.

- **No Fabricated Live Metrics:** The platform never invents artificial real-time queue lengths or fake waiting times.
- **Corroborated Observations:** Queue updates reflect reviewed submissions from field observers and voluntary citizen reports.
- **Configurable Staleness Policy:** Any queue update older than **30 minutes** (default threshold) is automatically stamped as:  
  `"No Recent Update — Please confirm with the centre."`
- **Zero Guarantee of Counter Processing Speed:** External factors such as state server connectivity, specific certificate verification procedures, and staff deployment remain under the exclusive control of official centre authorities.
